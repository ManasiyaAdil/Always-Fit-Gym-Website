Achieve a Healthy Weight Gain

🍽 Effective Weight Gain Strategies

Gaining weight in a healthy way requires a balance of proper nutrition, strength training, and consistency.

🥩 High-Calorie Diet

Eat nutrient-dense foods like lean meats, dairy, and nuts to increase caloric intake.

🏋 Strength Training

Focus on weightlifting to build muscle mass instead of gaining unhealthy fat.

🛌 Proper Rest

Aim for at least 7-9 hours of sleep to support muscle growth and recovery.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drinking enough water helps digestion and nutrient absorption for optimal weight gain.
