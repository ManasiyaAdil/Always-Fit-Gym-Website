🏋️‍♂️ Best Strength Training Strategies

Building strength requires a combination of **progressive overload, proper nutrition, and consistency**. Here are some effective strategies:

💪 Progressive Overload

Gradually increase weight, reps, or intensity to stimulate muscle growth and strength.

🍗 Protein-Rich Diet

Consume high-quality protein sources like chicken, eggs, and whey to support muscle repair.

⏳ Proper Rest & Recovery

Allow muscles to recover by getting enough sleep and taking rest days between workouts.

🏃‍♂️ Compound Exercises

Focus on multi-joint exercises like squats, deadlifts, and bench presses for full-body strength.
